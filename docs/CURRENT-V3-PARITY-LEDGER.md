## Disposition rules

Terminal dispositions are exactly:

- `ported`: current semantics were deliberately moved and have a production
  caller, target commit, and targeted GREEN evidence.
- `already-equivalent`: the official clean implementation independently covers
  the full source semantics with a production caller and targeted GREEN
  evidence.
- `legacy-only-retired`: evidence proves the item serves only an R-014.4
  exclusion, or the owner explicitly approved retirement.

Two non-terminal audit states prevent silent loss:

- `port-required`: current product behavior is absent or only partially
  represented in the official repository.
- `retirement-review`: the item may be extraction- or compatibility-only, but
  current evidence is insufficient for retirement.

Filename words such as `legacy`, `v1`, or `v2` are not sufficient by
themselves. For example, a currently supported provider wire protocol may use a
v1 schema name without being a legacy workflow-state reader.
